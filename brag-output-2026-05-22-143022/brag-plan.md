# Brag Plan: Abinivesh M — AI Product Engineer & SDE Portfolio

## What is this app?
A scroll-scrubbing cinematic portfolio website where a continuous film background plays full-screen while glass panels reveal About, Work, Skills, Resume, and Contact sections as you scroll — a single-shot cinematic portfolio built per the AI Character Portfolio guide.

## The angle
The portfolio *is* the product: one continuous shot, scroll-driven, with no cuts. The hook is the cinematic presentation itself — a personal site that feels like a trailer, not a document.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Abinivesh M" in giant Anton type on two lines (second line in the accent blue #3B82F6), slamming in against pure black, then the subtitle "AI Product Engineer & SDE" appears underneath.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The three stat cards landing one by one: "113" LeetCode, "3×" Hackathon wins, "2×" IIT finals — each with a soft card arrival sound
- Project chips appearing sequentially: NexusShield, SkillNexus AI, TrackX
- The "Let's build something." contact panel landing with email/GitHub/LinkedIn/LeetCode links

## Outro / punchline
The final panel: "Let's build something." with social links, then hold on the full composition for 1 second before cut to black.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: scroll-scrubbing film portfolio
- Interpretation: Wide shots, big type, dramatic reveals. Fewer scenes (4-5), longer holds. Confidence through restraint. Transitions are dramatic wipes and crossfades.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000 (pure black)
- Accent: #3B82F6 (blue)
- Text: #f5efe7 (off-white)
- Display font: Anton (headings)
- Body font: Inter (body copy)
- Strongest visual element: The glass panel (rgba(16,12,9,0.62) with backdrop-filter blur and 1px border) — the "film holds while section fades in" moment

## Share copy (draft)
My portfolio isn't a page — it's a film you scroll through.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: warm cinematic bed, low swell, fade under final logo
- Music treatment: start at 0.5s, fade out under final logo card, one dry logo hit on the name reveal
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time via npx hyperframes beats; target strong cues at ~3.0s (name reveal) and ~15.0s (final logo landing)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the hero glow and card presence breathe
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: typed text on name reveal (key ticks), beat-aligned stat card appearances, final logo hit
- Restraint rule: no waveform bars, no musical-note graphics, no heavy pulsing — motion and product story first
